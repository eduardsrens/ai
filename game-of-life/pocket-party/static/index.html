FRIENDS NOW. RIVALS IN A MINUTE.

A sunlit island. Big trouble.

A local pass-and-play party for 2–4 friends on one device. Choose 5, 8, or 12 rounds. Most trophies wins; keys break ties.

  1. Item first, then roll. Optionally trigger one item before rolling 1–6 (or 2–12 with Turbo Dice!).
  2. Claim shiny trophies. Pass any of the three Trophy Shrines and trade 20 keys for a trophy.
  3. Make it personal. Rockets deal 7 damage, Magnets steal 8 keys, Switcheroos swap positions, Turbo Dice double your roll, and Bubble Shields block one hit.
  4. Win the minigame. After each round, compete privately in Sweet Spot, Gem Scramble, Pocket Rush, or Star Snatch. Best score earns a trophy!
  5. Bounce back. At 0 HP, drop up to 8 keys and respawn at the Portal Gate with full 20 HP. Trophies stay safe.

Crossing Space 48 warps you through the Portal Gate back to Space 00. Progress saves automatically in local SQLite.
