FRIENDS NOW. RIVALS IN A MINUTE.

A small island. Big trouble.

A local pass-and-play party for 2–4 friends. Share one device. Play 8 rounds. Most trophies wins; keys break ties.

  1. Roll & roam. On your turn, optionally use one item, then roll 1–6 spaces. Collect keys, dodge traps, and find items.
  2. Aim for the shiny stuff. Pass one of three trophy chests and trade 20 keys for a trophy.
  3. Make it personal. Rockets deal 7 damage, magnets steal 8 keys, switcheroos swap positions. Shields block one hit. Use an item before rolling.
  4. Win the minigame. After everyone moves, each player gets a private attempt at a timing, memory, or tapping challenge. Best score earns a trophy (ties share it). Ranked rewards: 10, 7, 4, 2 keys.
  5. Bounce back. At zero health, lose up to 8 keys and respawn at the start with 20 HP. Your trophies are safe.

The end of the trail is a portal back to the start. Progress saves automatically. Hand off the device when prompted.
